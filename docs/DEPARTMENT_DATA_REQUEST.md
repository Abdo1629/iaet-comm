# IAET Electronics & Communications Website — Department Data Request

**Purpose:** This document lists the information and source files needed to turn the current multi-page website into an approved, accurate and maintainable official department website under an IAET subdomain.

**Important:** We have already collected the information that is publicly visible on the IAET website. Items marked “confirm/update” are not being requested from scratch; we need the department to verify that they are still correct and provide the latest approved version.

## 1. Department identity and official approval — essential

1. Official department/program name in Arabic and English exactly as it should appear.
2. Approved short name and acronym (for example, ECE) and any official logo/department mark.
3. Approved one-paragraph description of the department/program in both languages.
4. Name, academic title and official designation of the current Head of Department / Program Director.
5. The official date from which the current department identity and program name apply, if relevant.
6. Written approval to publish the department website as an official IAET subdomain.
7. The exact requested subdomain (for example, `ece.iaet.edu.eg`) and the IT contact responsible for DNS, SSL and hosting coordination.
8. The person or office authorized to approve website content and request changes.

**Already found publicly:** The official IAET website links to an Electronics & Communications Engineering Program page at `/comm`. The institute homepage describes IAET as established in 1997 and identifies it as part of the Egyptian Academy of Aviation Sciences / Ministry of Civil Aviation ecosystem. These are institute-level facts; please confirm the preferred official wording and links.

## 2. Head of Department / Program Director — essential

1. Approved welcome message / word from the Head of Department in Arabic and English.
2. Current professional portrait (high-resolution JPG/PNG) and permission to publish it.
3. Updated short biography (100–200 words) in both languages.
4. Academic degrees, awarding institutions and years.
5. Academic and administrative responsibilities / committees.
6. Research interests and selected publications with DOI or official publication links.
7. Official office email, office location, office hours and approved contact method.
8. CV file (PDF) if it is approved for public download.

## 3. Faculty members and supporting staff — essential

Please provide one current, approved spreadsheet for all department staff, separated into the categories used by IAET.

For each person, where approved for publication:
- Full official Arabic and English name.
- Academic title / job title.
- Department role and employment category.
- Highest qualification, institution and year.
- Specialization / research interests.
- Courses currently taught (if the department wants this published).
- Official institutional email and office/extension (if public).
- Short biography and CV (PDF), if available.
- High-resolution portrait and publication permission.
- Profile visibility status (public / internal only).

Please also identify staff who have left, changed title or moved to another role so old records can be removed or archived.

## 4. Academic program and regulations — essential

1. Latest approved internal regulations, including amendments and effective date.
2. Latest program specification (Arabic and English, if both exist).
3. Current study plan / academic map for every level or year.
4. Course list in Excel/CSV with:
   - Course code and official title (Arabic/English).
   - Level/year and semester.
   - Credit hours and contact hours.
   - Prerequisites / co-requisites.
   - Course type and department responsible.
5. Latest course specifications and course reports, preferably grouped by academic year.
6. Program learning outcomes / graduate attributes / competencies.
7. Assessment and examination framework approved for public explanation.
8. Graduation requirements and approved progression rules.
9. Field training / internship requirements, if applicable.
10. Latest academic calendar and registration windows.

**Already found publicly:** The existing program page links to internal regulations, program/course specifications and program competences. Some published academic links and schedules appear old or lead to restricted SharePoint files. Please send current direct files or a folder with view access.

## 5. Student services and academic resources — essential

1. Latest student guide.
2. Current lecture and laboratory schedules for all levels.
3. Current examination schedules and rules for absence / resits, if the department is responsible for publishing them.
4. Approved student forms (requests, petitions, project forms, training forms, etc.).
5. Academic advising process and assigned contacts.
6. Office hours / student reception arrangements.
7. Official links for student accounts, e-learning, academic email, library and student affairs.
8. Student support, complaints and suggestions procedure, including the correct official contact.
9. Student activities and technical clubs that the department wants represented.
10. Frequently asked questions approved by the department.

**Privacy boundary:** Student grades, personal records, account credentials and private academic data should remain in IAET's approved secure systems. The public website will link to those systems rather than duplicate them.

## 6. Laboratories, equipment and facilities — essential

For every laboratory:
1. Official Arabic and English lab name.
2. Purpose and technical specialization.
3. Courses / practical modules served.
4. Responsible academic or technical staff member.
5. Main equipment and software (model/type where relevant).
6. Approved capacity and access/booking rules.
7. Safety instructions and required PPE, if applicable.
8. High-resolution photographs and short videos, with permission to publish.
9. Approved captions and photo credits.
10. Any equipment or facility information that must remain unpublished.

Please provide an inventory spreadsheet and a folder of original images. We will not guess laboratory names or equipment from generic internet photographs.

## 7. Research, publications and graduation projects — essential

1. Latest approved departmental research plan and its validity period.
2. Approved research areas / research groups.
3. Selected staff publications with DOI or publisher links.
4. Research projects, funding or grants that may be publicly disclosed.
5. Partnerships, MoUs and research collaborations approved for public mention.
6. Graduation projects archive, grouped by academic year.
7. For each project: title, short abstract, year, student team, supervisor, technical field, image/demo/video link and permission to publish.
8. Awards, competitions and achievements with evidence and dates.
9. Student research and innovation activities approved for publication.

Please distinguish completed, ongoing and proposed projects.

## 8. Quality assurance and accreditation — essential

1. Current accreditation certificate(s), issuing body, scope, date and validity period.
2. Exact current accreditation status of the institute and, where applicable, the program.
3. Current quality assurance policy and department quality structure.
4. Approved program/course review process.
5. Annual program reports and course reports approved for publication.
6. Improvement plans and evidence that may be made public.
7. Current stakeholder surveys and feedback process.
8. Quality indicators / KPI definitions and approved values, if they are to be displayed.
9. Quality office contact and document owner.

**Already found publicly:** The IAET homepage currently states that the scientific departments are accredited by the National Authority for Quality Assurance and Accreditation of Education. The current program page refers to institute accreditation for academic year 2024/2025. Please provide the current certificate and exact wording before we publish any present-tense accreditation claim.

## 9. News, announcements and events — essential for ongoing operation

1. News archive for the last 2–3 academic years (titles, dates, summaries and links).
2. Upcoming events, seminars, workshops, visits and competitions.
3. Official photographs and videos with captions and dates.
4. Announcement categories and the person authorized to publish.
5. Official social media accounts and preferred links.
6. Editorial approval workflow and expected update frequency.

## 10. Alumni and career engagement — recommended

1. Approved alumni profiles / distinguished graduates with consent.
2. Verified graduate statistics and employment data, with source and year.
3. Employer partnerships and training agreements that may be disclosed.
4. Career events, job fairs and professional-development opportunities.
5. Alumni survey or contact form, preferably through an IAET-approved platform.
6. Any graduate success stories with evidence and permission.

No employment rate, graduate count or employer partnership will be published without evidence.

## 11. Contact, location and official links — essential

1. Dedicated department email (if available) and approved phone/extension.
2. Office location, building/floor/room and student reception hours.
3. Exact map pin or official Google Maps link.
4. Official institute website and correct Arabic/English page URLs.
5. Official e-learning, academic mail, student affairs, library and quality links.
6. Social media links managed by the department.
7. Contact form recipient and the office responsible for responding.
8. Emergency or urgent student contact route, if the department wants one displayed.

**Already found publicly:** IAET publishes `info@iaet.edu.eg`, `02 33594060`, and the address Al Matar, Imbaba, Giza, Egypt. These are institute-wide details, not confirmed department-specific contacts.

## 12. Website functionality and integrations — essential decisions

Please confirm which features are required at launch:
1. News and announcements managed by the department.
2. Search across pages and downloadable documents.
3. Filterable faculty directory and course catalogue.
4. Graduation project archive and gallery.
5. Download center for regulations, guides, schedules and forms.
6. Student enquiry form and its recipient.
7. Bilingual chatbot / digital assistant, and the approved source documents it may use.
8. Analytics access and the office/person allowed to view reports.
9. Content management approach: named department editors, update requests through the department office, or an existing IAET CMS.
10. Whether any section should be private/login-only. Private student data must use IAET-approved authentication and systems.

## 13. Arabic and English content review — essential

1. Approved Arabic and English wording for the department name, overview, vision, mission and objectives.
2. Preferred technical terminology and spelling.
3. English names and academic titles for all staff.
4. Approved translations of regulations, course titles and student guidance.
5. Person(s) responsible for reviewing both languages before publication.

## 14. Brand assets and media — essential

Please share original, high-resolution files where available:
- Official IAET logo in SVG/PNG and any brand guidelines.
- Approved department/program logo or emblem, if one exists.
- Official colors and typography, if documented.
- Department, laboratory and facility photographs.
- Head of Department and staff portraits.
- Approved event, student-project and graduation-project images/videos.
- Any video or presentation officially approved for the program website.

Please avoid screenshots of documents where the original PDF/Word/Excel file is available.

## 15. File delivery format

To make review and implementation easier, please send:
- PDF for signed/approved regulations, policies, reports and certificates.
- Excel for staff directories, course lists, schedules, laboratory inventories and KPI tables.
- Word for editable messages, biographies and descriptions.
- Original JPG/PNG/SVG for logos and photographs.
- A shared folder with view/download permission for the website development team.
- For every file: title, academic year, approval date, version number and responsible office.

## 16. Approval and content ownership

Please nominate:
1. One academic approver for department information.
2. One person to verify staff and academic records.
3. One person to provide news, events and media.
4. One IT contact for the subdomain, DNS, SSL and deployment.
5. One final approver for Arabic and English content before launch.

Please confirm how often the department wants schedules, announcements, staff information and academic documents reviewed.
